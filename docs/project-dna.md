## Purpose & Problem Statement

**Problem**: Users need a platform to connect and share content across social networks with seamless integration and Persian/RTL support.

**Solution**: A modern web application for social connectivity and content sharing built with Next.js 16, featuring Persian localization, interactive components, and real-time updates.

---

## Target Users

- **Primary**: Social media managers and content creators seeking integrated publishing workflows
- **Secondary**: Teams needing collaborative social media management tools
- **Tertiary**: Individual users wanting to maintain personal social presence across platforms
